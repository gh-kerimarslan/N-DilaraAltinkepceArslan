About The Artist

Dilara primarily questions life itself, and subsequently the concepts of the individual, society, culture, ethics, and belief within a framework of balance. The phrase “farz edelim ki / what if” has consistently served as the starting point of her thinking practices.

In her works, she processes raw or raw-like textile materials using techniques and symbols that have been employed for thousands of years, adopting an approach that remains independent from the calendar.

Since 2021, through the Ortak Mekik project led by Gülçin Aksoy, she has incorporated digital production into her practice. In these works, she creates alternative worlds through VR and AR, drawing on environments, objects and narratives familiar from the physical world.
