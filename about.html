About The Artist

Born in Bursa in 1993, Dilara Altınkepçe Arslan completed her undergraduate degree in Carpet, Kilim, and Traditional Fabric Patterns at Mimar Sinan Fine Arts University (MSGSÜ) in Istanbul. Between 2015 and 2016, she broadened her artistic scope by specializing in Costume and Accessory Design at the Accademia di Belle Arti di Bologna, Italy.

In 2024, she earned her Master of Fine Arts degree with her thesis titled "Investigation of the Animal Image as a Symbol in Anatolian Kilims". Dilara synthesizes ancient Anatolian weaving rituals with digital realities, interactive AR/VR installations, and spatial art—creating a continuous dialogue between collective human memory and the technologies of tomorrow.

"My work lies at the intersection of ancestral weaving rituals and the digital pixels of tomorrow. I aim to bridge the tangible threads of the past with the intangible virtual realities of the future."
