About

Dilara Altınkepçe Arslan is a contemporary conceptual artist whose practice bridges ancient textile traditions, Central Asian shamanic rituals, and spatial installations with modern new media technologies.

By weaving cotton threads onto raw fabrics and integrating virtual realities (VR), augmented reality (AR), and data-interactive sculptures, her work investigates collective unconsciousness, environmental collapse, and spiritual survival in the digital age.
