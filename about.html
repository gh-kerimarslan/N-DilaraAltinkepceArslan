About The Artist

Born in Bursa in 1993, Dilara Altınkepçe Arslan began her undergraduate studies in 2011 at Mimar Sinan Fine Arts University (MSGSÜ), within the Department of Carpet, Kilim, and Traditional Fabric Patterns. Between 2015 and 2016, she broadened her artistic scope by training in Costume and Accessory Design at the prestigious Accademia di Belle Arti di Bologna, Italy.

In 2024, she published her master's thesis titled "Investigation of the Animal Image as a Symbol in Anatolian Kilims," which heavily influenced her artistic philosophy. Dilara synthesizes traditional weaving rituals with contemporary media, digital art, and VR installations, reflecting a profound dialogue between ancient human memory and modern technology.

"My work lies at the intersection of ancestral weaving rituals and the digital pixels of tomorrow. I aim to bridge the tangible threads of the past with the intangible virtual realities of the future."
