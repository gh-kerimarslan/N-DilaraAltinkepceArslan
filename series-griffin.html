Mythological & New Media Collection

Griffin Series

Reimagining the ancient myth of the griffin as a modern symbol for ecological crises and technological transformation. Consisting of five distinct creature motifs—cheetah, gorilla, crow, molok, and cockroach—this series reflects humanity's evolving perception of divinity amidst drought, radiation, and digital acceleration.
