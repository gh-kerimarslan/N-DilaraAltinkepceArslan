Intestine War Series

This series approaches humans and animals not as separate beings, but as two opposing forces within the same world. In the tension between good and evil, right and left, reason and instinct, neither side prevails. The works present this encounter as a constantly shifting and continually renewed state of balance, with neither a winner nor a loser.
