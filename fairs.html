A Griffin in the Steppe

2023 | 72x52 cm | Noise Media Art

This griffin is a reflection of contemporary issues, most notably climate issues. Molok, Cockroach, Cheetah, Crow, and Gorilla respectively symbolize drought, radiation, speed, wisdom, and humanity. Some of these animals come together due to the absence of the concepts they represent, while others unite due to their harmful excessive presence.
