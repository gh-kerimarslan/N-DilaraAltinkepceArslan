The Ritual Series

"The Ritual Series" delves into repetitive actions, ancestral habits, and collective memory. Through the meticulous interlacing of threads, the artist deconstructs historical ceremonies and reconstructs them as contemporary spatial inquiries creating physical and spiritual portals between past and future.
