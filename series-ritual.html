The Ritual Series

"The Ritual Series" delves into the repetitive actions, ancestral habits, and collective unconscious that shape the human experience. Through the meticulous interlacing of threads, the artist deconstructs historical ceremonies and reconstructs them as contemporary spatial inquiries. Each piece in this series acts as a gateway—a physical and spiritual portal connecting past memories to modern existence.
