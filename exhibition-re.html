Solo Exhibition

RE-

2024 | İstanbul, Türkiye

The prefix "re-" implies a return, a repetition, and a reconstruction. In this comprehensive solo exhibition, Dilara Altınkepçe Arslan explores the cyclical nature of human existence, ancestral memory, and spatial confrontation. Through a multidisciplinary approach encompassing fiber art, suspended installations, and augmented reality (AR), "RE-" transforms the gallery space into a sanctuary of reawakening and introspection.
