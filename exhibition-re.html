Solo Exhibition • Nelumbo Studios • 2024

RE-

Fiber Art | Spatial Installation | Mixed Media

History is not behind us; it echoes through actions, thoughts, and memories woven into the present moment. Every passing second reverberates in our current lives, making it feel both strangely familiar and entirely new, reminding us of all the times that have been lived before.

"RE-" stands as a profound exploration of these cyclical patterns. The exhibition delves into the concept of ossified uprisings, silenced hopes, and the interconnected narratives that bind humanity. Through the meticulous process of contemporary fiber art and grand spatial installations, the artist physically ties the knots of past traumas and future possibilities.

"How many more days can I remain silent? My hands are no longer enough to keep them shut..."
