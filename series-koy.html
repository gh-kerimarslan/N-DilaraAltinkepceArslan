Anatolian Heritage & Shamanic Memory

Village (Köy) Series

Tracing the roots of nomadic Anatolian life, "Köy" explores the physical and spiritual geography of rural sanctuaries. Connecting raw straw, wool, and natural earth dyes, the artworks capture forgotten shamanic rites and ancestral hearths that continue to whisper through contemporary steppe landscapes.
