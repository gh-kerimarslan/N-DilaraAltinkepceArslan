Köy (Village) Series

The "Köy" (Village) Series is an ongoing exploration of communal memory, ancestral roots, and the shamanic heritage embedded within Anatolian and Central Asian cultures. Through intricate fiber art and conceptual mapping, this series reimagines the village not just as a physical space, but as a spiritual habitat where human consciousness and nature intertwine.
