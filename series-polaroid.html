Fiber Art

Polaroid Series

Capturing fleeting spiritual visions like instantaneous Polaroid frames, this series transforms sacred encounters, from the divine descent of Durga to the awakenings of spring, into lasting images.

Nelumbo Studios

Hıdırellez

2024 | 49x59 cm | Thread on raw cotton fabric
"I celebrated the arrival of spring with all my friends."
Cermodern, Nelumbo Studios

Durga's Visit

2024 | 49x59 cm | Thread on raw cotton fabric
"In the ceremony that we delivered our spring anniversary to Ud, I set my soul on the road and met with Durga in Sky. I consulted her for this cycle, which is the year of calmness, reason, and consciousness. Here is a picture from our meeting. Happy Ud Year!"
Cermodern & Nelumbo Studios

Awakened Anew

2024–2025 | 49x59 cm | Thread on raw cotton fabric
"Situations that occur outside the ordinary flow of life lay the foundations of self-construction. The form of this formation sometimes creates mega structures, and sometimes brings great destructions. Contrary to appearances, the difference between these two outcomes is incredibly close. On this razor-thin path lies an image, fading away, visible only to those with an insatiable hunger for life. An image that can be seen by those who, every time they look at the moon, sleep with the excitement of the sun that will rise afterward."
Nelumbo Studios

A Message from the Future

2024 | 49x59 cm | Thread on raw cotton fabric
"The soil gave no nutrients during the planting season, and many souls ascended to the Sky this year. I consulted the gods, but found no answer. I pursued my question across lands and from time to time. The guilt remains in my hands."
