Ortak Mekik

Traditional Weaving | Digital Synthesis | VR Experience

"Ortak Mekik" is a visionary research and design project conducted by Mimar Sinan Fine Arts University (MSGSÜ) and supported by the Istanbul Development Agency (ISTKA). It serves as a pioneering hub that brings together traditional weaving technologies and natural dyeing methods with advanced digital and interactive technologies.

For the artist, this project marks a profound turning point. It was within the scope of this visionary framework that her groundbreaking interactive work, "Anthrenus Verbasci", was first created and exhibited.

"A critical intersection where physical fiber art transcends its boundaries and steps into the boundless realm of virtual reality."

This collision of the ancient and the digital allowed the artist to weave narratives not just with threads, but with pixels, questioning the very fabric of reality and human perception in the modern age.
